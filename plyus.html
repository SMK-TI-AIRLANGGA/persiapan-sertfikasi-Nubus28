Plyus

Author: Riana

Description: Pylus is a story about the hidden paths of discovery, where every search leads to revelations that challenge our expectations. It reminds us that the answers we chase are often disguised, and the truths we uncover may not align with the hopes we carry. In this journey, characters learn that desire and reality rarely walk hand in hand; what seems certain can dissolve into mystery, and what feels lost can suddenly appear in unexpected forms. This book explores the paradox of pursuit: the more we seek clarity, the more we encounter surprises. Pylus is not just about finding—it is about understanding that the destination may differ from the dream, and that sometimes the most valuable treasures are those we never thought to look for
